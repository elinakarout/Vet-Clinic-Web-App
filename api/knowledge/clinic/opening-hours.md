# Opening hours

All times on this page are **clinic local time (Asia/Beirut)**. The booking
system stores every appointment in UTC and converts it back to clinic time for
display, so the times you see in the app are the times shown here.

## When we are open

The clinic is open **Monday to Friday**. We are **closed on Saturday and
Sunday**, and we do not offer routine appointments at weekends.

Consulting hours depend on which veterinarian you are booking with, because each
vet keeps their own schedule:

| Veterinarian | Speciality | Consulting hours | Appointment length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dr. Anita Patel | Small animal medicine | Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 | 30 minutes |
| Dr. Marek Novak | Surgery | Monday–Friday, 10:00–18:00 | 45 minutes |

Taken together, the clinic sees patients from **09:00 to 18:00, Monday to
Friday**. The earliest appointment of the day is with Dr. Patel at 09:00 and the
latest is with Dr. Novak, starting at 17:15.

## Finding an actual free time

The hours above are the *working* hours, not a promise that every slot is free.
Booked appointments, and any time a vet has blocked off for surgery, holiday or
lunch, are removed from the list automatically. The appointment booking page
always shows the real remaining slots for the day you pick.

## Outside opening hours

We do not run an overnight or weekend emergency service. If your pet has an
urgent problem when we are closed, contact your nearest 24-hour emergency
veterinary hospital straight away rather than waiting for us to open. See
*Emergency guidance* for the signs that mean "go now, do not wait".
