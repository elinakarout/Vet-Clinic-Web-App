# Booking and cancellation policy

## How to book

You can book online through your account or by calling the clinic during
opening hours. Online booking shows only slots that are genuinely free, so
anything you can see, you can have.

To book you need an account with your pet already added, because every
appointment is attached to a specific patient rather than to you. If the pet is
new to the clinic, add it to your profile first and pick it when you book.

## Appointment lengths

Appointment length is set by the veterinarian, not chosen by you:

- **Dr. Anita Patel — 30 minutes.** Routine consultations, vaccinations, health
  checks, medical problems.
- **Dr. Marek Novak — 45 minutes.** Surgical consultations, procedures and
  pre-operative assessments.

Slots start on a fixed grid, so Dr. Patel's appointments begin at 09:00, 09:30,
10:00 and so on, and Dr. Novak's at 10:00, 10:45, 11:30 and so on. You cannot
book a time that falls between grid slots.

## How far ahead you can book

You can book up to **one year ahead**. When browsing for a free time you can
look at up to **31 days** at once.

## One appointment at a time per pet

A pet cannot be booked with two veterinarians at the same time. If you need your
pet seen by both vets, book the appointments back to back rather than
overlapping.

## Cancelling and rescheduling

Please cancel as early as you can so somebody else can use the slot.

**Clients can cancel their own appointments up to 2 hours before the start
time.** Inside that final 2-hour window the system will not let you cancel
online — call the clinic instead and a member of staff will do it for you.
Clinic staff can cancel at any time, including same-day, so a genuine emergency
is never a problem.

To reschedule, cancel the existing appointment and book the new time. A
cancelled slot goes straight back into the pool and can be booked again
immediately, by you or by anybody else — so if you are moving an appointment to
a busy day, book the new time first where you can.

## Missed appointments

We do not currently charge for a missed appointment, but repeatedly missing
booked slots without cancelling may mean we ask for a deposit on future
bookings.

## Late arrival

If you arrive late we will still see you if we can, but we may have to shorten
the consultation so the next client is not delayed, or ask you to rebook. If you
know you are running late, call us.
