# Services and prices

All prices are in **US dollars (USD)** and are guide prices for a routine,
uncomplicated case. We will always confirm the cost with you before we begin
treatment. If a visit turns out to need more than was booked, we tell you what it
will cost and ask before going ahead.

Prices do not include prescription medication dispensed to take home unless the
entry says so.

not pay a separate consultation fee for a routine vaccination visit.

| Vaccination | Price |
|---|---|
| Dog — first puppy course (two visits) | 85 USD |
| Dog — annual booster (DHPP) | 55 USD |
| Dog — rabies | 40 USD |
| Dog — kennel cough (Bordetella) | 38 USD |
| Cat — first kitten course (two visits) | 80 USD |
| Cat — annual booster (FVRCP) | 52 USD |
| Cat — rabies | 40 USD |
| Cat — feline leukaemia (FeLV) | 45 USD |

A dog vaccination therefore costs **40 to 85 USD** depending on which
vaccination is due: 55 USD for the routine annual DHPP booster, 40 USD for
rabies on its own, and 85 USD for the complete two-visit puppy course. See
*Vaccination schedules* for what is due at what age.

## Dentistry

| Service | Price |
|---|---|
| Dental check and grading | 35 USD |
| **Dental cleaning (scale and polish, under anaesthetic)** | **180 USD** |
| Dental cleaning with extractions | from 240 USD |
| Single tooth extraction | from 45 USD |

A **dental cleaning costs 180 USD**. That price covers the pre-anaesthetic
check, the general anaesthetic, scaling, polishing and same-day recovery care.
Extractions are charged on top, because we cannot tell how many teeth are
affected until your pet is under anaesthetic and we have taken a proper look. We
always call you during the procedure before doing any extraction.
